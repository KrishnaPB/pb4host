# PB4HOST — free-channel promotion kit
**Prepared 13 October 2026. Status: drafts, not published.**

## Strategy
Start with WordPress speed evaluations as a low-friction conversation starter and white-label support as a higher-value agency offer. Promote emergency repair through useful problem-solving content, not fear or unsolicited claims that someone's site is hacked. Assumption: PB4HOST wants Indian and English-speaking international customers; refine after the owner confirms markets and delivery capacity.

“Free” means no required ad spend for the proposed activity, not no labor or guaranteed free listings everywhere. Platform rules, feature availability and account eligibility must be checked at execution. Trying every channel simultaneously is less useful than making three channels work.

**Launch gate:** fix the audit's checkout/pricing/SLA conflicts first; verify landing links and lead handling. Do not repeat “90+ Core Web Vitals,” instant repair, certification, client-count or uptime claims without evidence. Prices are intentionally omitted from the drafts pending reconciliation.

## Measurement
Use `utm_campaign=organic_launch_2026_10`, a consistent source, `organic_social` for social, `referral` for partner/directory traffic and `email` for opted-in newsletters. Give each post a unique `utm_content`. Never put names, emails, phone numbers, passwords or client domains into campaign parameters.

Suggested events (implementation not verified): `service_cta_click`, `whatsapp_click`, `lead_form_success`, `begin_checkout`, `purchase`. Fire form success only after server acceptance; purchase only after confirmed payment, deduplicated by transaction ID. A WhatsApp click is not a lead; a checkout start is not a sale. Follow applicable consent rules.

Weekly metrics:
- Sessions by tagged channel, where measurement is permitted.
- Qualified leads: real need, service fit, buying authority and workable timeframe/budget.
- Lead-to-sale rate = confirmed new customers / qualified leads.
- Revenue and gross margin from confirmed orders, not quoted values.
- Time per qualified lead = channel work hours / qualified leads. If denominator is zero, report “not yet measurable.”
- Median first response time and unresolved inquiries.

Direct messaging can lose attribution. Ask “How did you hear about us?” and record self-reported source separately. After two weeks establish a baseline; no visitor, ranking or sales increase is guaranteed.

Use `promotion-tracker.csv` privately. It is an empty template: do not commit customer contact information or lead records to a public repository.
